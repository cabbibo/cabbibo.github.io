Labmarket Clinical Trial Atlas

Twenty ways to see a clinical trial.

A set of GPU-rendered, self-hosted explanations of how global clinical trials actually work — from phase attrition and eligibility criteria to enrolment, supply, safety signals and the digital twin. Every one is interactive. Every one runs on a single shared study.

Self-hosted. No build step, no CDN, no network calls at runtime. three.js, the Archivo typeface and the world geometry are all vendored under assets/.
To run: double-click any file in viz/, or serve the folder (serve.cmd on Windows, serve.sh on macOS/Linux) and open http://127.0.0.1:8731.
Data. The study behind every page — LMK-204, 84 sites, 49 countries, 2,400 planned patients — is synthetic but modelled on real Phase III oncology parameters. Nothing here is patient data.
Keys. ← → move between visuals, Esc returns here.
